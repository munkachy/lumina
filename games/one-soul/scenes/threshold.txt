Your one picks him up.

That is not a figure of speech and there is no better way to say it. There is nothing to pick up with and no weight to it, and what happens is that the whole attention of a creature that has stood beside one man for forty-nine years is now applied to carrying him, and that is what carrying is.

*page_break

It does not judge him.

It has never judged anything. Not the candy on Liberty Avenue in 1980, not the finger, not the car outside Renee's mother's house in June of 1998, not one time in forty-nine years. It is not equipped for it and it has never wanted to be.

It carries him the way it has carried every prayer he ever said, including the four words he could not finish when he was nine.
