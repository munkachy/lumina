He is nineteen and at Pitt and living in a building on Semple Street with four other people and a shower that runs cold at seven.

And you have a problem, and the problem is that you have been trying for four months to get your one to stand on one particular thing, and every time it gets there, there is already something there, and it is never the same something.

*page_break

In September one of them introduces itself as Belphegor.

It is perfectly civil. It says that this is not really its area, that its area is more the long-term, and that what you want is probably the one who handles the immediate. It says it will pass it along.

In October a different one says it never got any message from Belphegor, and asks whether you are sure you spoke to Belphegor and not to someone who was covering. It says it will look into it.

In November one of them claims to be Asmodeus, which would be a very serious matter if it were true.

In December Belphegor is back and does not appear to remember September.

*page_break

"They're stalling," you say.

"Yes."

"How long have they been doing this?"

"Since 1975."

*page_break

"And every hour you spend on it —"

"Is an hour I am not standing where I should be. Yes." Your one says this without any bitterness, which somehow makes it worse. "The paperwork is not in the way of the attack. The paperwork *is* the attack."

*page_break

*comment ---- the recognition ----

And you start laughing, and you cannot stop, and your one waits.

"What."

"I worked twenty-nine years for Allegheny County," you say. "Twenty-nine years. Deeds, then assessments, then eleven years in the office where people came to complain about assessments."

"Yes."

"Do you know what this is? This is a *runaround.* This is the oldest, cheapest, stupidest thing in the world and I have watched it done four hundred times by men making eleven thousand dollars a year, and the entire trick of it is —"

You stop.

*page_break

"The entire trick of it," you say slowly, "is that they want you to think there are more of them than there are."
