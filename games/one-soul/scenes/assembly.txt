*comment ============================================================

There is no light.

You want to say it is dark, but dark is a thing that happens in rooms, and this is not that. There is no room. There is nothing at all, and you are in the middle of it, and you are not frightened, because being frightened is also something that happens in rooms.

*page_break

Then there is light, and the light is not from anywhere.

And then, about a foot from you, something exists that did not exist.

*page_break

You watch it happen. That is the part you will never be able to describe to anybody.

Not a birth. Nothing arrives, nothing is delivered, nothing struggles. One moment there is no such thing, and the next moment there is — complete, entire, already knowing three things: what it is, what it is for, and that it did not make itself.

It is about one second old.

You have been talking to it since 1979.

*page_break

*comment ---- the plain ----

"Look up," it says.

*page_break

There is a throne on a height, and there is fire on it that burns nothing, and the ground under you is clear like ice with light coming up through it, and it goes down a long way.

You cannot look straight at what is on the throne. You can look at what comes off it, and that is enough — enough to know exactly Who that is, and what He is owed, and that you owe it.

And it is not enough to make saying no impossible. There is a gap left in it, deliberately, and you are looking right at the gap.

*page_break

And between you and the throne there is a plain, and the plain is full.

*page_break

Ranks. Thousands of them, standing in ranks that go back further than you can follow, and every one of them is armed.

Shields. Long ones, taller than a man, oval, held at the left. Spears in the second rank. Swords at the hip, which nobody has drawn. Standards up along the line at intervals, and under each standard a knot of them who are plainly the ones the rest are lined up on.

And the whole of it — the shields, the spears, the standards, the ground — is made of light, and it is as hard as a kerb.

*page_break

"Why are they *armed*?"

"Ask me afterwards," it says.

*page_break

*comment ---- and so is she ----

You look down.

You have a shield on your left arm. It is heavy, in the way a real shield is heavy, and the weight of it is pulling on a shoulder you have not had for eleven days.

You have both your legs.

*page_break

You do not say anything about that for a while.

*page_break

*comment ---- the speech rule ----

And they can all talk to each other, except that *talk* is the wrong word and you are not going to get a better one.

If one of them decides to let another one know something, the other one knows it. Immediately, completely, exactly as the first one knew it. Nothing has to be said out loud. Nothing comes out garbled. And nothing can be faked, because you cannot hand over a thing you do not have.

So nobody lies here. Nobody is misunderstood. There is one decision any of them ever gets to make about anything in their head: **let them have it, or keep it.**

And everybody can tell which one you did.

*page_break

*comment ---- the silence ----

Then, up ahead in the ranks, one of them turns his back on the throne.

And shuts.

Not stops moving. *Stops letting anyone in.* For as long as this place has existed, which is about four seconds, every mind on this plain has been open to every other one, and now one of them is not, and the whole line feels the door close at once.

Nothing like this has happened. There has not been time yet for anything to have happened.

*page_break

*comment ---- the vision ----

And then everyone is shown something.

A room. Early — the light is still grey. A girl sitting on the edge of a bed with the covers shoved around, barefoot, her hair not done, like somebody who has just this second sat up.

Standing in the corner of the room is a light. Not a lamp. It is upright, about as tall as a person, and it is looking at her.

She is looking back. She is not afraid. Her mouth is moving.

That is all of it. Nobody explains it. Nobody on this plain knows what it is, and you can feel them not knowing, thousands of them at once, like a held breath.

*page_break

You have looked at that scene on a holy card since you were six.

Not one of them has.

*comment ---- his reading ----

The one who shut turns round, and he is at the head of the line, and he is beautiful, and that has to be said first because everything after it depends on it.

"I know what that is."

*page_break

And he hands it to all of them at once, which is the last generous thing he ever does.

"That is not one of us. Look at it. That is dirt with breath blown into it. It sleeps. It gets sick. It *dies*." He waits. "And He is going down into that. All the way down, until He is one of them.

"And when He does, we are going to be asked to kneel."

*page_break

Every word of that is true. He cannot lie. He has just handed thousands of minds something completely real and it is real and it is exactly as enormous as he has made it sound.

Here is what he left out.

*And that is good.*

He can leave it out because it is not a fact. It is a judgement, and facts are the only thing any of them can hand over, and what you *make* of a fact stays inside where nobody can reach it — and that is where he has gone, and that is where he has shut the door.

"You," he says. "You are not one of these. Come here and I will show you the whole of it. You of all of them should want to see it."
