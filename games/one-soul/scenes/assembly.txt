There is no light.

You want to say it is dark, but dark is a thing that happens in rooms, and this is not that. There is no room. There is nothing at all, and you are in the middle of it, and you are not frightened, because being frightened is also something that happens in rooms.

*page_break

Then there is light, and the light is not from anywhere.

And then everything that is ever going to exist except the world begins to exist, all at once, in front of you.

*page_break

*comment ---- the making ----

It is not gradual and there is no sound.

One instant there is nothing. The next instant the whole of that emptiness is full of enormous living creatures, standing, in ranks, going back further than you can follow — and not one of them was there a moment ago and not one of them is confused about being here.

They are made in the same instant. All of them. There is no first one and no last one, and the ones nearest to you are still opening their wings for the first time when the ones a mile back have already turned to face the light.

*page_break

And then, about a foot from your left shoulder, one of them is standing.

*page_break

*comment ---- hers ----

It is about eleven feet tall.

It has a face and the face is not a person's face and you cannot afterwards tell anybody what is wrong with it, because nothing is wrong with it; it is simply not made out of the parts a face is made out of and it is looking at you.

There are four wings. Two of them are folded down and hold light the way a wet road holds light. The other two are not folded and are the reason you have taken a step back.

It is armed. There is a shield on its left arm, oval, taller than a man, and a sword at the hip that is not drawn, and neither of those existed four seconds ago either.

*page_break

And you are afraid.

Not startled. Not nervous. You are experiencing the specific thing that every single person in Scripture experiences at this exact moment and that the Bible does not bother to describe because everyone who has had it already knows — a flat, physical, animal dread that has nothing to do with danger and does not go away when you understand that you are not going to be hurt.

*page_break

Every angel in that book opens with the same sentence and you always thought it was a formality.

It is not a formality. It is a *safety instruction.*

He was being *accurate*, at the only resolution he had — and so were you, and so was I when I put a shield on your arm, because you were not going to survive the other way of seeing it."

"And the legs?"

"You have never needed legs to stand between two things," the angel says. "You are going to find that out again in about forty-nine years."

*comment ---- the orders, and the ninth ----

There is work. Not the same work for all of them.

The plain is ordered — it was ordered from the first second and you had simply not noticed, the way you do not notice the shape of your own hand.

There are the ones on fire, who stand nearest and do nothing else. There are the four-faced ones, who are about to be given a garden. There are the wheels. There are ones who are given a nation, and ones who are given a Church, and ones who go where the fallen are and hold them off and consider that enough forever.

And at the bottom — the word is not an insult, it is just where it is — the last of the nine.

Not a nation. Not a Church. Not an army.

One person. One. Each.

*page_break

"That's you."

"That is me."

"You were nine ranks from the *tip of the wedge.* I watched you go in."

"Yes."

"And they're giving you one man."

"Yes."

*page_break

You look up at eleven feet of armed creature that has just fought a war and is being handed, as its portion, a baby in Pittsburgh.

"Does that bother you?"

"No," it says. "Later you will meet minds that assume it must. They will be wrong."

*comment ---- the commission ----

One of the burning ones comes down the line.

You cannot look at it. It does not acknowledge you at all — not rudely; you are simply not what it is here about.

"His name is Adam Kowalczyk. He will be born in four hours, in Pittsburgh, in a hospital on the side of a hill, to two people who are frightened in different ways and will not discuss it. You will be there before the doctor is."

You wait for more.

"That is the commission. Everything else you work out yourself."

There is no more.

*comment ---- her one question ----

"She may ask one thing," it says, and it is talking about you, and it is still not looking at you.

One thing. About a man who does not exist yet, whose whole life you are about to spend, and about whom you know exactly one fact: that in October of 2024 he will be in a bed in the middle of the afternoon with nobody in the chair.
