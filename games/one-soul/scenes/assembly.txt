There is no light.

You want to say it is dark, but dark is a thing that happens in rooms, and this is not that. There is no room. There is nothing at all, and you are somehow in the middle of it, and you find that you are not frightened, because being frightened is also something that happens in rooms.

*page_break

Then there is light, and the light is not from anywhere.

And then, about a foot from you, something exists that did not exist.

*page_break

You watch it happen. That is the part you will never be able to describe to your sister. Not a birth — nothing arrives, nothing is delivered, nothing struggles. One moment there is no such thing, and the next moment there is, complete, entire, already knowing three things: what it is, what it is for, and that it did not make itself.

It is about one second old.

You have been talking to it since 1979.

*page_break

*comment ---- 1.2 : the throne ----

"Look up," it says.

You look up, and there is a throne.

There is fire on it that does not burn anything. The floor under you is clear, like ice with light coming up through it, and you can see down into it a long way.

You cannot look straight at what is on the throne. You can look at the light that comes off it, and that is enough — enough to know exactly Who that is, and what He is owed, and that you owe it. You have never in your life been so certain of anything.

And it is not enough to make saying no impossible. You notice that immediately. There is a gap left in it, on purpose, and you are looking right at the gap.

*page_break

"Why does it look like a throne?" you say. "Is it a throne?"

"You are a human being," it says. "You were given eyes and a room to put things in. Everyone who has ever been shown this has said *throne*, and *fire*, and *a floor like glass*, and every one of them was reaching, and every one of them was right."

*page_break

*comment ---- 1.3 : the court, and the harps ----

And you are not alone.

The place is full. Thousands of them, standing in what you can only call ranks, and it takes you a while to work out what is strange about it, and then you have it:

No two of them are the same kind of creature.

Later, down there, you will remember that everything alive comes in flocks — ten thousand sparrows, and every last one of them a sparrow. There is nothing like that here. God made one of these and stopped. Then He made one of something else and stopped again. All the way down the room. Every mind standing on this floor is the only one of its kind that has ever existed or will.

You look for a long time.

"Not one of them has a harp," you say.

"No."

"Not one."

"No."

*page_break

*comment ---- 1.4 : the speech rule ----

And they can all talk to each other, except that *talk* is the wrong word and you will not have a better one.

Here is how it works. If one of them decides to let another one know something, the other one knows it. Immediately. Completely. Exactly the way the first one knew it. Nothing has to be said out loud. Nothing comes out garbled. And nothing can be faked, because you cannot hand over a thing you do not have.

So nobody lies here. Nobody is misunderstood. There is only one decision any of them ever gets to make about anything in their head: **let them have it, or keep it.**

And everyone can tell which one you did.

*page_break

*comment ---- 1.5 : the silence ----

Then one of them, up ahead in the ranks, turns his back on the throne.

And shuts.

Not stops moving — *stops letting anyone in.* For as long as this place has existed, which is about four seconds, every mind in it has been open to every other one. Now one of them is not. And all of them feel the door close at the same instant, and so do you, and it goes through you the way a crack goes through ice you are standing on.

Nothing like this has happened.

There has not been enough time yet for anything to have happened.

*page_break

*comment ---- 1.6 : the vision ----

And then everyone is shown something.

*image annunciation.webp A room at dawn. A young woman sitting on the edge of a rumpled bed. A column of gold light standing in the corner.

A room. Early — the light is still grey. A girl is sitting on the edge of a bed with the covers shoved around, barefoot, her hair not done, like somebody who has just this second sat up.

Standing in the corner of the room is a light. Not a lamp. It is upright, and it is about as tall as a person, and it is looking at her.

She is looking back. She is not afraid. Her mouth is moving.

That is all. That is the whole thing. Nobody explains it. Nobody in this place knows what it is, and you can feel them not knowing, thousands of them at once.

*page_break

"That is the test," your one says quietly. "They are being shown one thing and asked to say yes to it without being told what it means."

"What is it?"

"You know what it is."

You do. You have looked at that scene on a holy card since you were six. Nobody here has. Not one of them.

*page_break

*comment ---- 1.7 : his reading ----

The one who shut turns around.

"I know what that is."

And he lets all of them have it at once, and that is the last generous thing he will ever do.

"That is not one of us. Look at it. That is dirt with breath blown into it. It sleeps. It gets sick. It dies." He waits. "And He is going down into that. All the way down into it, until He is one of them. And when He does, we are going to be asked to kneel."

Every word of that is true.

He cannot lie. None of them can. He has just handed all of them something completely real, and it is real, and it is every bit as enormous as he has made it sound.

*page_break

Here is what he left out.

*And that is good.*

He could leave it out because it is not a fact. It is a judgement. Facts are the only thing any of them can hand over. What you *make* of a fact stays inside, where nobody can reach it, and that is where he has gone, and that is where he has shut the door.

*page_break

*comment ---- 1.8 : she is put inside it ----

"Tell him he's wrong," you say. "Go on. Tell him."

"I cannot."

"Why not?"

"Because this already happened," it says, "and because this one was mine. It is the only choice I ever made that nobody could make for me and nobody could take from me, and it is the reason there is anything here for you to shape at all."

You start to argue.

"But you can see what it was like," it says. "There is only one way to see that."

*page_break

You are in the ranks.

You are not watching any more. You are standing in it, and the floor is under you, and the light is on you, and the question is being put, and it is being put to *you*.

They hold where they are, and behind them are the ones who have not chosen yet, and the line of light has to take the floor back one rank at a time.

You are in the line.

*page_break

Something comes at you.

It cannot hurt you. You cannot hurt it. You are simply locked, the two of you, on the same object, and neither of you can move, and behind you the mind you are standing on is running out of time to decide.

*comment ---- 1.11 : it is over, and none of it was hers ----

Then it is over.

A third of the lights in the place go out at once. Not thrown, exactly — though *thrown* is the word that gets used later and it is close enough. They turned away from the only thing holding them up, and they kept turning, and there was nowhere else to go.

And where they were is not dark. It is worse than dark. It is a shape in the middle of the light that the light does not go into.

*page_break

You are back beside it. Whatever you were standing on, you are not standing on it any more.

"None of that was you," it says. "You understand that. It happened before you existed, and it happened to me, and I chose what I chose, and nothing you just did changed one thing about it."

"Then why —"

"Because now you know what it was."

*page_break

And then, after a moment:

"And because now I know what *you* would have done."

*comment ---- 1.12 : the orders, and the ninth ----

There is work. Not the same work for all of them.

The place is ordered — it was ordered from the first second and you simply had not noticed, the way you do not notice the shape of your own hand.

There are ones who stand nearest the fire and do nothing but burn. There are ones who hold the whole of what is and turn it over forever. There are ones who are given a nation. There are ones who are given a Church. There are ones who go where the fallen are and hold them off and consider that enough for eternity.

And at the bottom — the word is not an insult here, it is just where it is — the last of the nine.

Not a nation. Not a Church. Not an army.

One person. One. Each.

*page_break

"That's you," you say.

"That is me."

"That's the *bottom.*"

"Yes."

You look at it for a while, this thing you have been talking to for forty-five years, which has just watched a third of everything that exists throw itself away and has been handed, as its portion, one person.

"Does that bother you?"

"No," it says. "Later you will meet minds that assume it must. They will be wrong."

*page_break

*comment ---- 1.13 : the commission, and her one question ----

One of the great ones comes.

You cannot look at it either. It does not acknowledge you at all — not rudely; you are simply not what it is here about.

"His name is Adam Kowalczyk," it says. "He will be born in four hours, in Pittsburgh, in a hospital on the side of a hill, to two people who are frightened in different ways and will not discuss it. You will be there before the doctor is."

You wait for more.

"That is the commission. Everything else you work out yourself."

There is no more.
