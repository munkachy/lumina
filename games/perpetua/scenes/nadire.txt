*comment ---------------- The two jobs ----------------

There are exactly two things that can be done and they are the same object.

**One.** A ship can grapple the upper end of the falling section and burn east, hard, for seventy-one minutes, and drag the whole nine hundred kilometres far enough round the world that it lays down in the Sonder Ocean instead of across Kell, Ottery, and the Fold Works. After seventy-one minutes the geometry is gone and the section is committed.

**Two.** A ship can put shuttles on Station Nine and take nineteen people off. It takes ninety minutes: the platform's dock is rated for one shuttle at a time and there are nineteen people and a hard vacuum and no lift.

You cannot do both. Nobody can do both. Under acceleration the platform's dock cannot be approached at all, and ninety minutes of rescue puts the burn window nineteen minutes in the past.

There are two ships in this system.
