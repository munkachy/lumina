The graving dock at Sarn Basin is kept at four degrees to save power, and your breath is going out in front of you in the gallery lights.

Through the glass she is eighty metres of scuffed grey and one long welded seam where somebody, years ago, put a hole in her and somebody else closed it. Two of her hull letters have been repainted and do not match. There is a scaffold up against her dorsal sensor block with nobody on it, because it is the third watch and the yard is asleep.

CS *Perpetua*. Meridian class. Twenty-six years old. A hundred and twelve berths. Yours in nine hours.

A dock hand goes past behind you pushing a cart with one bad wheel and does not look up. On the gallery wall there is a board with the ship's particulars stencilled on it, and a line at the bottom that reads MASTER: VACANT, and somebody has written a name over the blank in pencil and then rubbed most of it out again.

You can still read it. It says Halloran.

Fourteen months ago Ivo Halloran took this ship into the Ceyla system, disobeyed an order to hold and observe, and came out with nine hundred and six colonists in his cargo bays. The Survey held an inquiry. The inquiry did not publish a finding. Halloran retired at his own request, and the *Perpetua* has been in dock ever since, and now she is being given to you.

You have nine hours. You could spend them asleep in the transit hostel, like a sensible officer.

*comment ---------------- Reading in ----------------

At 1100 you stand on the quarterdeck of your own ship and read the commissioning order aloud to a hundred and twelve people who are standing very straight and have been at sea longer than you have.

It takes ninety seconds. The words are two hundred years old and most of them are about the Concord and none of them are about you. At the end you say *I relieve you* to a duty officer you have never met, and she says *I stand relieved*, and that is all there is to it. There is no music. Nobody claps. Somebody at the back coughs.

Then a hundred and twelve people look at you, and it is 1101, and the rest is yours.
