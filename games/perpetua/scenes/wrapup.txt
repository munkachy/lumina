The *Perpetua* was surveyed at Marl Colony and again at Sarn Basin.

*if (strain >= 70)
  The finding was that the number three grapple mount and eleven metres of primary frame were beyond economical repair on a twenty-six-year-old Meridian, and that the cost of putting her right exceeded her replacement value by a factor of four.

  She was paid off eleven months later. The Survey does not scrap Meridians; it sells them, and she went to a Sarnese ore consortium who took the guns off, put a bigger reactor in, and are running her still.

  The plaque came off the bulkhead outside the bridge before she went, as the regulation requires, and was sent to the Survey's naming board on Ilium, where it sits in a drawer with two hundred and nine others waiting for a hull.
*if (strain >= 40) and (strain < 70)
  The finding was that she could be repaired and that it would take nineteen months in a yard she could not reach under her own power.

  She was towed to Sarn Basin, which took eleven weeks, and she came out of the graving dock with a new frame section, a number three grapple mount from a class that had not been built for eleven years, and a number two coolant loop that still whistles.
*if (strain < 40)
  The finding was that she had been worked hard and honestly and would go on for another decade.

  She came out of the Marl yard in seven weeks with a straightened mount, two new frame members, and a number two coolant loop which the Marl yard also failed to stop whistling, bringing the total number of yards in the Concord that have failed to stop it whistling to eleven.

The *Perpetua* was in commission for twenty-six years before you and for as long afterwards as she was allowed, and eleven masters had her, and you were the ninth.

She was eighty metres of scuffed grey with one long welded seam where somebody put a hole in her and somebody else closed it, and she yawed to port under braking because of that weld, and her number two coolant loop whistled at exactly the pitch of a kettle, and every yard in the Concord failed to stop it.

You had her for a year.
