You know now that the number two coolant loop whistles at exactly the pitch of a kettle, and that after four days you stop hearing it and after seven you start hearing it again, worse. You have walked the ship at 0300 twice. Both times somebody was already awake and doing something reasonable, which is either an excellent sign or evidence that word travels.

Ahead, at the end of the leg, is Halbard: a grey moon around a gas giant nobody has bothered to name properly, with three hundred and forty people living in an ice mine on the terminator, and a mail bag for them in your hold.
