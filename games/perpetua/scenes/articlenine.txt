*comment ---------------- Article Nine ----------------

Article Nine of the Survey's disciplinary code covers unauthorised disclosure. It is one paragraph long and it gives a ship's master exactly three roads, and it does not say which.

**Charge.** Log it, hold him, hand him to a Board at the next member colony. Dismissal is usual. It is out of your hands the moment you sign it, and it is on the record for a hundred years.

**Summary.** Deal with it aboard under a master's authority. Disrate him, stop his pay, put him in the bilges for a month — anything short of dismissal. It stays on the ship. It also means that if it ever comes out, you concealed a hundred and nineteen counts of Article Nine.

**Dismiss the matter.** Find that no offence occurred. This is available to you and it is a lie, and it is a lie you would have to sign.
